Welcome to Dylan's Gallery

Summary about us

Dylan's Gallery is a custom design tattoo parlour located in Pakhurst but we are more than that. We pride ourselves in being a place where you can come and bring all your ideas
to life in our comfortable, atmospheric and creative environment.
As a team of artists we hold ourselves to the highest professional standards and in doing so we will not only help you design artworks you want to carry with you but we will also
make sure that you feel safe and comfortable by ensuring a clean and fun environment.
All your tatoo and design needs can be found in our parlour and brought to life by our artists who are well-versed in all tattoo styles and aesthetics. We look forward to seeing you!

Summary of Our Services

What we do here is very simple. We want to bring your vision to lo life with our exceptional tattoo artistry.
We design your tattoo for you, all we need from you is to tell us what you want and if you don't know,
don't worry because our team is here to work with you every step of the way.
