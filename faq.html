Frequently Asked Questions

Here you will be able to find answers to some of your concerns and if you don't feel free to Contact Us

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

the answer varies from person to person. It also depends on where you are getting the tattoo on your body and the size of it. Everyone's pain tolerance is different so you won't know until you try!

How do I choose the right design?

We have a group of experienced artists who can assist you with this but the important thing is that you choose a design that speaks to you and think about things like the size of the tattoo, the placement and how it makes you feel.

How do I prepare for my appointment?

Make sure you get enough sleep, have something to eat before you embark on this experience. Avoid alcohol a day before and the day after getting your tattoo and don't overthink it.

What is the aftercare process like?

Our artist will go through everything with you after you get your tattoo. Keep the wrapping on for 3-4 hours after getting your tattoo. Do not scratch or pick at your tattoo. Avoid direct sunlight and soaking the tattoo in water. Clean your tattoo after the first 3-4 hours and then twice a day thereafter. Make sure to use fragrance free and sensitive skin soap like Dove. We recommend using Baby Epi Max lotion after each wash.

How much does it cost?

You can refer to our pricing guidelines below and on our Homepage page. Those prices may vary depending on the size, complexity and whether you would like things like coloured ink on your tatoo. But don't worry you will get a clear breakdown of the quote after you work with one of our artists on your design.

Can I get a tattoo if i am pregnant or breastfeeding?

It is advised that you wait until after pregnancy and breastfeeding.

What should I not do before my appointment?

Avoid alcohol, caffeine or any blood thinning medication if it can be helped.

How do I book an appointment?

You fill out the form on our Contact Us page. You will then be contacted by one of our artists to send through your design, thereafter you will receive your appointment information by Email.
